A tiny peek under the hood

So, how does CBN work?

Concept Bottleneck Models make an AI's intermediate concepts visible, giving people a way to inspect and sometimes correct what the model believes it sees.

STEP 01 / INPUT
🖼️

See the thing

The model receives an input, such as an image of a bird, car, clothing item, or fruit.

STEP 02 / CONCEPTS
🧩

Spot the clues

It predicts human-readable concepts — like beak shape, roof height, sleeve length, or fruit texture.

STEP 03 / PREDICTION
🎯

Make a call

A final classifier uses those concepts to produce a category. Inspecting the concepts can help reveal where a prediction went wrong.
